## Transportation Lane (/scmtms/tl5):
A transportation lane represents a direct, valid connection between two locations (or Zones) in you supply chain network.  

It tells the system that "Yes, it is possible to move goods from location A to B".  

If no **transportation lane** exists between two locations, VSR optimizer fails to plan the shipment because it assumes there is no valid path.  

### Note: Manual planning is still possible.

## Lane defines:
**Source Location:** Start location.  

**Destination Location:** End location.

**Means of Transport:** Type of vehicle allowed on the lane.  

**Carriers:** List of carriers operates the lane.  

**Transit Time & Distance:** Distance of the lane and time taken to move the shipment.  

**Planning cost:** A "cost penalty" used by the optimizer to decide which lane to use for planning.
