# Plant in TM
An ERP plant is mapped 1:1 to a **TM location** (/n/scmtms/loc3).  


### Plants act as anchor for the transport:
* **Outbound Process:** The plant is the source location.  
* **Inbound Process:** The plant is the destination location.

### Shipping point connection:
**In SAP TM**, we often map the ERP **Shipping point** to the TM location as the plant. This ensures that when ERP order is created with specific shipping point, TM knows where the goods are starting from.  


In case of **ASR (Advanced Shipping and Receiving)**, shipping point is used as the destination location in freight unit.  


For **Non-ASR**, if you want to use shipping point as destination location you can switch on **Improved location determination** check box in Logistic Integration Profile.  


For MM scheduling agreements, if address is changed in the address tab, and it differs from the storage locations' address, a new **one time location** will be created as the destination location in freight unit.
