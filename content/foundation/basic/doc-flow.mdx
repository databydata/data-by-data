## Business Models

Understanding the role of the business entity is the first step in configuring the supply chain.

### Shippers
Shippers are companies that **manufacture** goods and sell them directly in the market. They own the production lifecycle and manage the outbound logistics of their finished products.

### Retailers
Retailers do not manufacture goods. Instead, they **procure** products from different manufacturers and sell them in the market. Their logistics focus is heavily on inbound procurement and store distribution.
