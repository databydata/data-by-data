## Transportation Zone (/scmtms/zone)
Zones are grouping of multiple locations into a single logical entity used to simplify planning.  

Instead of creating thousands of transportation lanes for every single customer address, you group them into a zone.   
(e.g., "Northeast Region" or "Postal Codes 10000–19999").  

You then define lanes, rates, and carrier assignments for the entire zone, which significantly reduces master data maintenance.  

## Types of zones include:

**Direct Zone:** Explicitly lists specific locations (e.g., a list of 5 specific vendors).

**Postal Code Zone:** Defined by a range of zip codes.

**Region Zone:** Defined by a Country, State, or Region.
